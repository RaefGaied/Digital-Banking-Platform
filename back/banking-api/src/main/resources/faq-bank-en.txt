Q: How can I make a bank transfer?
A: Provide the amount and the destination account, then confirm the action when the assistant asks for approval.

Q: How do I report fraud?
A: Report the situation through support and a human verification will be triggered.

Q: What happens if my card is blocked?
A: Never share your card details. Contact support from the app to verify and secure the card.

Q: How can I check my balance?
A: You can check your balance from the Account section or by asking the banking assistant.

Q: How can I pay or withdraw money?
A: Specify the amount and recipient, then confirm the operation when the assistant asks for approval.

Q: How can I update my profile?
A: Open the Profile section and follow the modification steps. Sensitive data may require extra validation.

Q: How can I contact support?
A: Use the banking chat or create a support ticket from the Support section. Sensitive requests may be routed to a human agent.

Q: How do I access my card limits?
A: Check the Cards section to view the available limit and latest card information.

Q: How do I manage my investments?
A: Review the investment products in the Investments section and choose the option best suited to your needs.
